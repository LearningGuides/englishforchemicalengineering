# 🏭 Integrated Case Study

## Background

A chemical manufacturing plant produces an industrial solvent used in coatings and cleaning products.

Recently, engineers observed several operational problems:

- 📉 Production decreased by 12%.
- ⚡ Energy consumption increased.
- 🌡️ Reactor temperature fluctuated during operation.
- 🧪 Laboratory tests showed variations in product purity.
- ⚙️ A circulation pump experienced frequent vibration.
- 🌬️ Emissions from one process unit approached the plant's operating limits.

Management formed a multidisciplinary engineering team to investigate the situation.

---

## Step 1: Collecting Information

The engineering team reviewed:

- Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs)
- Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs)
- Production reports
- Laboratory analyses
- Maintenance records
- Instrument calibration records
- Energy consumption reports
- Environmental monitoring reports

Accurate documentation provided a complete picture of plant performance.

---

## Step 2: Identifying the Problems

The investigation revealed several contributing factors:

- Heat exchanger fouling reduced heat transfer efficiency.
- An improperly calibrated temperature sensor caused unstable reactor control.
- The circulation pump showed signs of bearing wear.
- A valve did not open completely, reducing flow rate.
- Product losses increased because of process variability.

Rather than having one single cause, the problems were connected.

---

## Step 3: Engineering Discussion

The engineering team held a technical meeting.

During the discussion, team members:

- Reviewed operating data.
- Compared laboratory results.
- Shared maintenance observations.
- Evaluated several improvement options.
- Considered safety and environmental impacts.
- Prioritized corrective actions.

Each recommendation was supported by technical evidence.

---

## Step 4: Implementing Solutions

The plant implemented several improvements:

- Cleaned the heat exchanger.
- Recalibrated the temperature sensor.
- Replaced the worn pump bearings.
- Repaired the control valve.
- Updated operating procedures.
- Improved preventive maintenance schedules.
- Increased process monitoring using digital dashboards.

Each action addressed one or more identified problems.

---

## Step 5: Results

After the improvements:

- 📈 Production returned to normal.
- ⚡ Energy consumption decreased.
- 🌡️ Reactor temperature became more stable.
- 🧪 Product purity consistently met specifications.
- ⚙️ Pump reliability improved.
- 🌬️ Emissions decreased.
- 💰 Operating costs were reduced.
- 🌱 Overall process sustainability improved.

This case study demonstrates how different engineering disciplines work together to solve complex industrial problems.

# 📖 Technical Vocabulary Review

The following table reviews important vocabulary from this course.

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Material Balance | Accounting of material entering and leaving a process. |
| Heat Transfer | Movement of thermal energy between systems. |
| Mass Transfer | Movement of chemical species from one location to another. |
| Reactor | Equipment where chemical reactions occur. |
| Distillation | Separation process based on differences in boiling points. |
| Catalyst | Material that increases reaction rate without being consumed. |
| Heat Exchanger | Equipment that transfers heat between fluids. |
| Pump | Machine used to move liquids through a process. |
| Flow Rate | Volume or mass of fluid moving per unit of time. |
| Calibration | Adjustment and verification of instrument accuracy. |
| Process Control | Automatic regulation of process variables. |
| Quality Control | Activities that ensure products meet specifications. |
| Sustainability | Efficient use of resources while minimizing environmental impact. |
| Digitalization | Integration of digital technologies into industrial operations. |
| Predictive Maintenance | Maintenance based on equipment condition and performance data. |
| Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) | Document describing the correct way to perform a task. |
| Batch Record | Complete documentation of a production batch. |
| Hazard | Source of potential harm. |
| Corrective Action | Action taken to eliminate the cause of a problem. |
| Continuous Improvement | Ongoing effort to enhance processes and performance. |
